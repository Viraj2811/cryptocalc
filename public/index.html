      How the crypto buy calculator works

      When you buy crypto, the money you pay is not the money that buys coins. The calculator follows your cash through each step so the final number matches what you can expect in your wallet.

      1. Your payment method may take a charge first, a percentage, a flat amount or both.
      2. The exchange then takes its trading fee from what is left, plus 18% GST on that fee if you turn it on.
      3. The remaining money buys coins at the price you entered, adjusted for any spread or slippage.

      Switch to "I want a coin amount" to work backwards: enter how much crypto you want and see the cash you need to pay.

      Frequently asked questions

      Why do I get less crypto than the money I pay?

      Several small costs come out of your payment before coins are bought: the payment charge from your bank or card, the exchange trading fee, GST on that fee in India, and the spread between the quoted price and the price you actually trade at. This calculator adds them all up so you can see the real amount.

      What is spread or slippage?

      Spread is the gap between the price to buy and the price to sell. Slippage is when a larger or fast order fills at a slightly worse price than the one you saw. Both make your effective price higher than the quoted price.

      What is the difference between maker and taker fees?

      A maker adds an order to the order book, usually a limit order that waits to be filled. A taker fills an existing order immediately, as with a market order. Taker fees are usually higher, and the calculator uses the fee rate you enter.

      Is GST charged on crypto purchases in India?

      Indian exchanges charge 18% GST on their trading fee, not on the value of the coins you buy. Turn on the GST option to include it. TDS and income tax on selling are not calculated here.

      Where does the price come from?

      Live prices come from CoinGecko and can differ slightly from the order book on your exchange. You can type your own price at any time.

      Does this calculator store my data?

      No account is needed. Your choices are saved only in your own browser so they are there next time. See the privacy page for details.
