Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This page is a plain-language template. Have a qualified lawyer review it for your country before you launch.

The short version

You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, bank details or wallet address. Please never type any of those into the calculator.

What is stored on your device

Your last-used settings (coin, currency, fees and GST choice) are saved in your browser's local storage so they are ready next time. This stays on your device. You can clear it from your browser settings.

Price requests

To show live prices, your browser asks our server, which asks CoinGecko. Our server keeps your IP address in memory for up to one minute to limit abuse, and does not store it afterwards. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs under its own policy. If our server is unavailable, your browser may contact CoinGecko directly, and CoinGecko's privacy policy then applies.

Analytics

If analytics is switched on, we use Plausible, a privacy-focused tool that does not use cookies or collect personal data. It counts visits, pages and clicks on exchange links.

Affiliate links

Links to exchanges may be affiliate links. When you click one you leave this site, and that exchange's own privacy policy applies. We never see what you do there beyond a click count.

Children

This tool is meant for adults. It is not aimed at anyone under 18.

Changes and contact
